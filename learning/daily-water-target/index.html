Daily Water Target

SARX PICKHIGH EVIDENCE

PERSONALIZED TO YOU

DOSE½ oz / lb bodyweight
TIMINGDaily

Half your bodyweight in fluid ounces is a reliable, individualized baseline. Adjust upward in heat, at elevation, or with high training volume. This formula scales better than fixed volume recommendations like '8 glasses per day'.

🌅 Front-load mornings

you wake up mildly dehydrated after 7–8 hours without water. Plasma volume drops during sleep, affecting blood pressure, cognition, and morning alertness. 16–32 oz first thing restores plasma volume before coffee compounds the mild diuretic effect.

👁️ Easiest way to check

urine color. Pale yellow throughout the day means you're on track. Dark yellow or amber means drink more. Clear urine can indicate overhydration. Aim for pale yellow, not clear.

🥗 Water-rich foods count

approximately 20% of total daily fluid intake comes from food. Cucumber (96% water), celery (95%), watermelon (92%), and strawberries (91%) contribute meaningfully toward your target. Whole foods hydrate in a way processed foods do not.
