Apple Cider Vinegar

TREND WARNINGTREND
DOSE1 tbsp
TIMINGWellness fad
Trend Warning: This is currently popular online but has limited or mixed clinical evidence. Use caution before adding it to your routine.

The primary claimed mechanism — alkalizing the gut — doesn't survive contact with stomach acid. The small human studies that exist are underpowered, short-term, and often manufacturer-funded. The raw apple cider version contains trace 'mother' bacteria, but far less than any fermented food.
